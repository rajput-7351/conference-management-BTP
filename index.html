      A Letter from the Conference Chair

      To all our esteemed colleagues, authors, and reviewers,

      Welcome to the official platform for our academic conference. I am Vishnu Tomar, the Conference Chair, and it is my distinct honor to introduce this tool.

      This conference management tool was designed from the ground up to solve a simple problem: to make the process of submitting, reviewing, and publishing academic work as seamless and efficient as possible. We wanted to move beyond clunky email chains and spreadsheets and create a single, transparent, real-time environment for everyone.

      What makes this project truly special is its origin. This platform is not the product of a single institution but a testament to the power of collaboration. We have brought together talented students and faculty from different colleges, each bringing unique perspectives and skills to the table.

      This ambitious project was made possible through our foundational partnership with LNMIIT. Their leadership, resources, and commitment to innovation were the driving force that turned our shared vision into a reality. We are incredibly grateful for their support.

      Whether you are an author submitting your latest research, a reviewer lending your expertise, or a track chair managing the process, this tool was built for you. We are excited to see the incredible work that will be shared through this platform.

      Sincerely,

      Vishnu Tomar
      Conference Chair
